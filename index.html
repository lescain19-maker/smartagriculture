Connect your ESP32

Use the device Auth Token from Blynk Console → Devices → your device → Device Info. The ESP32 must already be connected to Blynk with these datastreams.

Your token stays in this tab’s memory and is sent directly to Blynk over HTTPS. Closing or reloading the page clears it.

Blynk datastreams

In your Blynk template, create these virtual datastreams and ensure your firmware writes the sensor readings to the same pins.

SensorPinRange
Soil moistureV40–100 %
Soil temperatureV0−40–125 °C
Air temperatureV5−40–125 °C
HumidityV30–100 %
Water pump (integer)V60 = off, 1 = on

V6 must be handled by your firmware using BLYNK_WRITE(V6). Relay GPIO and active level depend on your wiring. Pump commands are enabled only while the device is online. Network or browser restrictions may prevent direct Blynk API access.
